# ABC Corporation — Return and Damage Policy (v2.0 — CURRENT)

## 1. Purpose
This policy governs the process for returning damaged, defective, or incorrect goods to suppliers, and replaces v1.0 in full.

## 2. Scope
Applies to all inbound goods received at ABC distribution centers found to be damaged, defective, or non-conforming to specification, and to goods discovered defective post-putaway during downstream quality checks.

## 3. What Changed From v1.0
- Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) requests are now submitted automatically through the ABC Supplier Portal rather than by email, reducing average RMA turnaround from 6 days to under 2 days.
- Return notification window shortened from 5 business days to **2 business days** to reduce inventory holding of damaged goods.
- Added a Quality Hold escalation path for defects discovered after putaway (not present in v1.0).
- Introduced tiered cost responsibility based on supplier tier (see Section 5).

## 4. Return Authorization Process

1. Warehouse identifies damaged/defective/non-conforming goods (at receiving per SOP-WH-IN-001, or post-putaway via Quality Hold tagging per POL-QA-004).
2. Warehouse Operations logs the issue in the ABC Supplier Portal within **2 business days** of discovery, attaching photographs and the GRN reference number.
3. Supplier Portal auto-generates an RMA number for Tier 1 and Tier 2 suppliers (per POL-SUP-002 tiering) within 24 hours; Tier 3 suppliers require manual Procurement follow-up.
4. Goods are packaged per the supplier's return packaging guidance (available in the Supplier Portal) and returned via standard carrier.
5. Credit note or replacement is issued by supplier upon receipt and inspection of returned goods, tracked to closure in the Supplier Portal.

## 5. Timeframes and Cost Responsibility

| Supplier Tier | Notification Window | RMA Turnaround Target | Return Shipping Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | 2 business days | 24 hours (auto) | Supplier-borne |
| Tier 2 | 2 business days | 24 hours (auto) | Negotiated, default 50/50 |
| Tier 3 | 3 business days | 3–5 business days (manual) | Case-by-case |

- ABC bears full return shipping cost if the notification window is missed, regardless of tier.
- No return accepted after 30 days from original delivery date unless a longer window is specified in the supplier contract.

## 6. Documentation Required
- Photographs of damage or non-conformance
- Original packing slip
- GRN reference number
- Quality Hold tag reference (if applicable, for post-putaway discoveries)
