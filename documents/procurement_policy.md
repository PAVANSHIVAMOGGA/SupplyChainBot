# ABC Corporation — Procurement Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the standards and controls governing the purchase of goods and services by ABC Corporation ("ABC") from external suppliers. It applies to all business units, including Manufacturing, Warehousing, and Retail Operations.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees involved in requisitioning, sourcing, negotiating, or approving purchases on behalf of ABC, regardless of purchase value.

## 3. Purchase Authorization Thresholds

| Purchase Value (USD) | Approval Required | Additional Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Up to $5,000 | Department Manager | None |
| $5,001 – $25,000 | Department Director | Two competing quotes |
| $25,001 – $100,000 | VP of Procurement | Three competing quotes + Finance sign-off |
| Above $100,000 | Chief Procurement Officer + CFO | Formal RFP process, legal review |

## 4. Supplier Selection Criteria

All suppliers must be evaluated against the following weighted criteria before a purchase order is issued:

1. **Price competitiveness** (30%)
2. **Quality track record** (25%) — based on defect rate history where available
3. **Delivery reliability** (20%) — on-time delivery percentage over trailing 12 months
4. **Financial stability** (15%) — verified via D&B rating or equivalent
5. **Compliance & sustainability** (10%) — supplier code of conduct acceptance required

New suppliers scoring below 60/100 on the composite scale require escalation to the Sourcing Risk Committee before onboarding.

## 5. Purchase Order Process

1. Requisition submitted via ABC Procurement Portal.
2. Budget availability confirmed by Finance.
3. Supplier selected per Section 4 criteria (or existing approved vendor list).
4. Purchase Order (PO) generated and approved per Section 3 thresholds.
5. PO transmitted to supplier; order acknowledgment required within 3 business days.
6. Goods Receipt Note (GRN) generated on delivery, matched against PO (3-way match: PO, GRN, Invoice) before payment release.

## 6. Emergency Procurement

In cases of production-critical shortages, an Emergency Purchase Order (EPO) may be issued bypassing the standard quote requirement, subject to:
- Verbal approval from Department Director, followed by written confirmation within 24 hours.
- Mandatory post-hoc review by Procurement within 5 business days.
- EPOs are capped at $50,000 per incident. Anything above requires CPO approval regardless of urgency.

## 7. Prohibited Practices

- Splitting a single purchase into multiple smaller POs to circumvent approval thresholds is strictly prohibited.
- Employees may not accept gifts, hospitality, or incentives from suppliers exceeding $75 in value without disclosure to Compliance.
- Sole-sourcing (single-supplier, no competitive process) requires written justification and VP approval regardless of value.
