# ABC Corporation — Supplier Onboarding Policy

## 1. Purpose
To ensure new suppliers meet ABC's standards for quality, compliance, financial stability, and ethical conduct before being added to the Approved Vendor List (AVL).

## 2. Onboarding Stages

### Stage 1 — Initial Screening
- Supplier submits company profile, business license, and references.
- Procurement performs a basic compliance check (sanctions list screening, business registration validation).
- Suppliers operating in high-risk jurisdictions (per ABC's Trade Compliance list) undergo enhanced due diligence.

### Stage 2 — Documentation Review
Required documents:
- Certificate of Incorporation
- Tax registration / VAT certificate
- Insurance certificates (general liability minimum $1M)
- Signed ABC Supplier Code of Conduct
- Quality certifications where applicable (ISO 9001, ISO 14001, IATF 16949 for automotive-grade suppliers)

### Stage 3 — Quality Audit
- Suppliers providing direct materials or finished goods undergo an on-site or virtual quality audit conducted by ABC Supplier Quality Engineering.
- Audit scoring uses the standard ABC Supplier Audit Checklist (0–100 scale). Minimum passing score: 70.
- Suppliers scoring 70–84 are onboarded with a Corrective Action Plan (CAP) and a follow-up audit within 6 months.
- Suppliers scoring below 70 are rejected and may reapply after 12 months.

### Stage 4 — Financial Risk Assessment
- Finance reviews credit rating, payment history (if existing relationship), and financial statements for suppliers with anticipated annual spend above $250,000.
- Suppliers flagged as high financial risk require a parent company guarantee or advance payment terms rather than standard net-30/net-60.

### Stage 5 — System Setup and Approval
- Supplier record created in ERP with unique Vendor ID.
- Payment terms, tax classification, and approved commodity categories configured.
- Final approval requires sign-off from Procurement, Supplier Quality, and Finance.

## 3. Ongoing Supplier Monitoring
Once onboarded, suppliers are re-evaluated on a rolling basis:
- **Quarterly**: on-time delivery and defect rate scorecards.
- **Annually**: full requalification audit for Tier 1 suppliers (suppliers providing components directly used in finished goods).
- Suppliers falling below performance thresholds for two consecutive quarters are placed on a Supplier Improvement Plan (SIP); failure to improve within two SIP cycles results in removal from the AVL.

## 4. Supplier Tiering

| Tier | Definition | Audit Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | Direct material / component suppliers, single-source risk | Annual |
| Tier 2 | Indirect materials, packaging, MRO supplies | Biennial |
| Tier 3 | Services (logistics, facilities, staffing) | On renewal only |
