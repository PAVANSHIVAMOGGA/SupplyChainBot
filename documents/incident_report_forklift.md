# ABC Corporation — Incident Report

**Incident ID:** INC-2026-0142
**Date of Incident:** 2026-01-22
**Location:** Distribution Center 03 (DC-03), Loading Bay 7
**Reported By:** Shift Supervisor, DC-03
**Classification:** Property Damage — No Injury

## Summary

At approximately 14:20 local time, a forklift operator reversing from Dock Door 7 collided with a stationary pallet rack, causing partial collapse of the top shelf. Approximately 40 units of SKU ABC-RM-2210 (raw material — packaging film rolls) were damaged and written off. No personnel were injured. The dock was closed for 3 hours during cleanup and rack inspection.

## Root Cause Analysis

1. **Immediate cause**: Operator did not perform a full visual check of the reverse path; a spotter was not present despite bay congestion protocol requiring one when visibility is obstructed.
2. **Contributing factor**: Dock Door 7's rear-view mirror had been reported faulty three days prior (Maintenance Ticket #MT-4471) but had not yet been repaired.
3. **Systemic factor**: Bay 7 has recurring congestion during the 2:00–3:00 PM wave due to overlapping inbound and outbound schedules, increasing near-miss frequency in this specific dock.

## Corrective Actions

| Action | Owner | Due Date | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Repair/replace rear-view mirror on affected forklift unit | Facilities Maintenance | 2026-01-24 | Completed |
| Retrain all DC-03 forklift operators on spotter protocol | Safety Officer | 2026-02-01 | Completed |
| Reassess Bay 7 wave scheduling to reduce congestion overlap | Warehouse Ops Manager | 2026-02-28 | In Progress |
| Update SOP-WH-IN-001 to reinforce spotter requirement language | Document Control | 2026-02-15 | In Progress |

## Cost Impact
- Damaged inventory write-off: approximately $6,200
- Rack repair: approximately $2,100
- Lost dock productivity (3-hour closure): estimated $1,800
