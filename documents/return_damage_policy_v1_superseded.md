# ABC Corporation — Return and Damage Policy (v1.0 — SUPERSEDED)

> **Note:** This version was superseded on 2025-09-01. See POL-RTN-003 v2.0 for the current policy. Retained for historical reference only.

## 1. Purpose
This policy governs the process for returning damaged, defective, or incorrect goods to suppliers.

## 2. Scope
Applies to all inbound goods received at ABC distribution centers found to be damaged, defective, or non-conforming to specification.

## 3. Return Authorization Process

1. Warehouse identifies damaged/defective goods during receiving (per SOP-WH-IN-001).
2. Warehouse notifies Procurement via email within 48 hours.
3. Procurement contacts supplier to request a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number.
4. Once RMA is received, goods are packaged and returned via standard carrier.
5. Credit note or replacement is issued by supplier upon receipt of returned goods.

## 4. Timeframes
- Return notification to supplier: within 5 business days of receipt.
- RMA must be requested within 10 business days of damage discovery.
- No return accepted by most suppliers after 30 days from original delivery date.

## 5. Cost Responsibility
- Return shipping cost is negotiated case-by-case with the supplier.
- ABC bears the cost if the RMA request is submitted after the supplier's stated return window.

## 6. Documentation Required
- Photographs of damage
- Original packing slip
- GRN reference number
