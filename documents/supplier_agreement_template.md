# ABC CORPORATION — SUPPLIER AGREEMENT (TEMPLATE)

**This Supplier Agreement ("Agreement")** is entered into as of [EFFECTIVE DATE] by and between ABC Corporation, a company incorporated under the laws of [JURISDICTION] ("ABC" or "Buyer"), and [SUPPLIER LEGAL NAME], a company incorporated under the laws of [SUPPLIER JURISDICTION] ("Supplier").

## Article 1 — Scope of Supply
Supplier shall manufacture, supply, and deliver the goods described in **Exhibit A (Product Specifications)** in accordance with the terms of this Agreement and any Purchase Orders issued by ABC under it.

## Article 2 — Pricing and Payment Terms
2.1 Pricing shall be as set out in **Exhibit B (Pricing Schedule)**, fixed for the initial term unless otherwise agreed in writing.
2.2 Payment terms are net [30/45/60] days from invoice date, contingent on successful 3-way match (Purchase Order, Goods Receipt Note, Invoice) per ABC's Procurement Policy.
2.3 Late delivery penalties, where applicable, are specified in Exhibit B.

## Article 3 — Delivery and Lead Times
3.1 Supplier shall maintain a standard lead time of [X] days from PO acknowledgment to delivery, as specified in Exhibit A.
3.2 Supplier shall notify ABC's Procurement team in writing within 24 hours of becoming aware of any anticipated delay exceeding 10% of the agreed lead time.
3.3 Delivery shall be [FCA / DAP / EXW — Incoterms 2020] at [DESIGNATED FACILITY].

## Article 4 — Quality Standards
4.1 Goods supplied must conform to the specifications in Exhibit A and pass inspection per ABC's Quality Control Checklist (POL-QA-004).
4.2 Defect rate shall not exceed [X]% of any given shipment, measured per the acceptance sampling plan referenced in POL-QA-004.
4.3 Supplier shall maintain relevant quality certifications (e.g., ISO 9001) for the duration of this Agreement and provide evidence upon request.

## Article 5 — Compliance
5.1 Supplier represents and warrants compliance with all applicable trade, labor, environmental, and anti-corruption laws.
5.2 Supplier agrees to abide by ABC's Supplier Code of Conduct, incorporated herein by reference.
5.3 Supplier shall promptly disclose any change in ownership, sanctions exposure, or material adverse financial condition.

## Article 6 — Term and Termination
6.1 This Agreement shall remain in effect for [X] years from the Effective Date, renewable by mutual written agreement.
6.2 Either party may terminate for material breach upon 30 days' written notice, provided the breaching party fails to cure within that period.
6.3 ABC may terminate immediately upon Supplier's insolvency, sanctions listing, or repeated quality failures resulting in Supplier Improvement Plan non-completion (per POL-SUP-002).

## Article 7 — Liability and Indemnification
7.1 Supplier shall indemnify ABC against third-party claims arising from defective goods, IP infringement, or Supplier's breach of applicable law.
7.2 Neither party shall be liable for indirect or consequential damages, except in cases of gross negligence or willful misconduct.

## Article 8 — Governing Law and Dispute Resolution
8.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of [GOVERNING JURISDICTION].
8.2 Disputes shall first be subject to good-faith negotiation, followed by binding arbitration under [ARBITRATION BODY] rules if unresolved within 60 days.
