# ABC Corporation — Trade Compliance and Customs Policy

## 1. Purpose
This policy sets out ABC's obligations and internal controls for the import and export of goods, ensuring compliance with applicable customs regulations, trade sanctions, and tariff classifications across all jurisdictions in which ABC operates.

## 2. Harmonized System (HS) Classification
- All SKUs must have a valid Harmonized System (HS) code assigned before any cross-border shipment.
- HS classifications are reviewed annually by Trade Compliance, or immediately upon introduction of a new product line.
- Misclassification discovered post-shipment must be reported to Trade Compliance within 24 hours and may require voluntary disclosure to customs authorities depending on jurisdiction and materiality.

## 3. Restricted and Sanctioned Parties
- All new suppliers, customers, and logistics partners must be screened against the applicable denied-party lists (including OFAC SDN, EU consolidated list, and UK sanctions list) prior to onboarding.
- Screening is repeated quarterly for all active trade partners.
- Any positive or partial match must be escalated to Legal before any transaction proceeds. No exceptions.

## 4. Country of Origin Determination
- Country of Origin (COO) must be determined per substantial transformation rules for goods undergoing manufacturing or assembly across multiple countries.
- COO documentation must accompany all shipments crossing an international border and be retained for a minimum of 5 years (7 years for shipments to jurisdictions with extended audit windows, e.g., EU).

## 5. Tariff and Duty Management
- Preferential trade agreement claims (e.g., USMCA, other applicable FTAs) require supporting certificates of origin on file before the preferential rate is applied.
- Duty drawback claims are managed centrally by Trade Compliance; business units must not file independent claims.

## 6. Recordkeeping
Minimum retention periods:

| Document Type | Retention Period |
|---|---|
| Import/export declarations | 5 years |
| Certificates of origin | 5 years |
| Denied-party screening records | 5 years |
| Voluntary disclosure filings | 10 years |

## 7. Restricted Commodities
ABC does not import or export the following without prior Legal and Trade Compliance written approval, regardless of customer request:
- Dual-use technology items subject to export control classification
- Items destined for comprehensively sanctioned countries
- Goods requiring end-use certificates where the end-use cannot be verified

## 8. Violations
Any employee who knowingly facilitates a trade compliance violation is subject to disciplinary action up to and including termination, independent of any regulatory penalty imposed on ABC.
